# Digital Business Card Generator

This app replaces the need for a printed business card. 
You can create a digital business card and share it with others.

## MVP Features
- Create a card: Set up your professional profile with contact details.
- Scan a card: Capture contact information from other users via QR code.
- Manage your card: Edit your information or delete your card entirely.
- Share your card: Show your unique QR code for others to scan.



## Roadmap
- SSO signup
- Customize card theme
- Export scanned cards to phone contacts
- Scan printed business card 
- Add contextual notes and tags to connections.
- Support for multiple card profiles.
